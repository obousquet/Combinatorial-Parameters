Addressing

Name: Addressing

Symbol:
\[\text{Add}\]
Definition:

Following [maass1992lower], let $r=\lceil\log_2 n\rceil$. The addressing class has $n$ concepts on a domain consisting of $n$ address points and $r$ bit points: the concept indexed by $i\in[n]$ is positive on address point $i$ and, on the bit points, equals a fixed injective $r$-bit encoding of $i$. In particular it combines a singleton family with its binary address.
