Anchored Torsion Class

Name: Anchored Torsion Class

Symbol:
\[\mathcal R_{\mathrm{tor}}\]
Definition:

Let $V=\{1,\ldots,6\}$ and let $(F_1,\ldots,F_{10})=(123,124,135,146,156,236,245,256,345,346)$, where a three-digit word denotes the set of its three digits. On the eleven-coordinate domain $X=\{x_0,x_1,\ldots,x_{10}\}$, define seven binary concepts $\mathcal R_{\mathrm{tor}}=\{h_a\}\cup\{h_v:v\in V\}$ as follows. The concept $h_a$ is zero on every coordinate. For $v\in V$, set $h_v(x_0)=1$ and, for $1\le i\le10$, set $h_v(x_i)=0$ if $v\in F_i$ and $h_v(x_i)=1$ otherwise. Thus coordinate $x_0$ separates the added concept from the other six, and the zero side of $x_i$ is $\{h_a\}\cup\{h_v:v\in F_i\}$.

Comments:

A finite strict witness for coefficient-field dependence of Yang dimension: value four in characteristic two, and three in characteristic zero or any odd characteristic. This single class does not give an unbounded-ratio separation. The construction is recorded with a direct integral-homology proof; no claim of published origin or minimality is made.
