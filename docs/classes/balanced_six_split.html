Balanced Six-Split Class

Name: Balanced Six-Split Class

Symbol:
\[\mathcal B_6\]
Definition:

Let $[6]=\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}$ and let $X=\{A\subseteq[6]:|A|=3,\ 1\in A\}$. Thus $X$ contains one representative of each unordered partition of $[6]$ into two triples and has ten coordinates. The balanced six-split class is $\mathcal B_6=\{h_v:v\in[6]\}\subseteq\{0,1\}^{X}$, where $h_v(A)=\mathbf1_{\{v\in A\}}$. The ambient domain is exactly $X$; no other coordinates are present.

Comments:

A finite obstruction to proper message-free unlabeled compression: no such scheme exists, even without stability or a size restriction. The general shattered-pair counting obstruction is proved in survey Proposition prop:proper-unlabeled-pair-obstruction. No novelty or minimality claim is made. Labeled or improper schemes are not excluded.
