Ben-David--Eiron Fixed-VC Self-Directed Family

Name: Ben-David--Eiron Fixed-VC Self-Directed Family

Symbol:
\[\mathcal C_n^d\]
Definition:

Fix $d\ge3$. Let $C_0^d$ be the $2^d\times d$ matrix whose rows are all binary strings of length $d$. Recursively, if $C_{n-1}^d$ has $r$ rows and $s$ columns, define $C_n^d$ as the $3\times3$ block matrix $$\begin{pmatrix}C_{n-1}^d&J&0\\0&C_{n-1}^d&J\\J&0&C_{n-1}^d\end{pmatrix},$$ where $J$ and $0$ are respectively the all-one and all-zero $r\times s$ matrices. The rows of this matrix are the concepts in $\mathcal C_n^d$ on its $d3^n$ column coordinates.

Comments:

Lemma 1 of Ben-David--Eiron proves $\mathrm{VC}(\mathcal C_n^d)=d$ and self-directed complexity at least $n$.
