Chen Neighbor Puncture

Name: Chen Neighbor Puncture

Symbol:
\[\mathcal N_{\mathrm{CCT}}\]
Definition:

Let $\mathcal C_0\subseteq\{0,1\}^{12}$ be the 100-word base class at $n=1$ in the definition of Chen–Cheng–Tang Teaching Products, and put $h=\mathtt{000001010101}\in\mathcal C_0$. Coordinates are $1,\ldots,12$, read left to right. For each coordinate $i$, let $h^{(i)}$ agree with $h$ except at $i$, where its bit is flipped. The Chen Neighbor Puncture is the finite class $$\mathcal N_{\mathrm{CCT}}=(\mathcal C_0\setminus\{h\})\cup\{h^{(i)}:1\le i\le12\}.$$ Repeated words are included only once. The nine defining seeds of $\mathcal C_0$ are owned by the Chen–Cheng–Tang Teaching Products record, not duplicated here.

Comments:

A fixed finite class, not an asymptotic family. Its construction is a local modification of the base in [chen2016recursive], not a class attributed to that paper. The database verifier reads both authoritative class definitions.
