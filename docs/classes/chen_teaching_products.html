Chen–Cheng–Tang Teaching Products

Name: Chen–Cheng–Tang Teaching Products

Symbol:
\[\mathcal C^{\mathrm{CCT}}_n\]
Definition:

Let $\mathcal C_0\subseteq\{0,1\}^{12}$ consist of all cyclic rotations of the nine words $\begin{gathered}\mathtt{000001010101},\ \mathtt{000001110101},\ \mathtt{000011010101},\\ \mathtt{000101110101},\ \mathtt{000111010101},\ \mathtt{001101010101},\\ \mathtt{001101110101},\ \mathtt{010101110111},\ \mathtt{011101110111}.\end{gathered}$ Word positions are the coordinates $1,\ldots,12$, read left to right; repeated rotations are included only once. For each integer $n\ge1$, define $\mathcal C^{\mathrm{CCT}}_n=\mathcal C_0^n$ on the disjoint-block domain $\{1,\ldots,n\}\times\{1,\ldots,12\}$: a concept independently chooses a word of $\mathcal C_0$ on each of the $n$ blocks. Binary words are identified with their sets of coordinates labeled one.

Comments:

The base class is Figure 4 of [chen2016recursive]; its first eight rotation orbits have twelve elements each and the last has four, giving 100 concepts. Products yield the fixed ratio $\mathrm{RTD}/\mathrm{VC}=5/3$, not an unbounded ratio. The authoritative seed words are stored only in this definition; sync/verify_chen_teaching_products.py checks the construction and can compare them with the durable primary-source extraction.
