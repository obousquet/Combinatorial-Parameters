Full Cube

Name: Full Cube

Symbol:
\[\{S:S\subset [n]\}\]
Definition:

For $n\geq1$, the full cube on domain $[n]=\{1,\ldots,n\}$ is $2^{[n]}$, the class of all $2^n$ binary labelings of $[n]$.
