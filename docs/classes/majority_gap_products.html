Products of the Majority-Query Separation Class

Name: Products of the Majority-Query Separation Class

Symbol:
\[C_{maj}^{t}\]
Definition:

For $t\ge1$, take the $t$-fold Cartesian product of $C_{\mathrm{maj}}$ on disjoint blocks of four coordinates. A concept chooses independently one of the eight $C_{\mathrm{maj}}$ strings on each block.
