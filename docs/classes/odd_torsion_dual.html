Odd-Torsion Dual Class

Name: Odd-Torsion Dual Class

Symbol:

Definition:

Comments:

The name refers to the Alexander dual of the completed complex, which is the whole-class nonspanning ambiguity complex. The class has Yang dimension ten in characteristic three and nine in every other characteristic. It is a strict finite reverse witness to the characteristic-two example, not an unbounded-ratio separation. The construction and proof are local; no novelty or minimality claim is made.
