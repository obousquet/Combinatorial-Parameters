Private-Coordinate Cubes

Name: Private-Coordinate Cubes

Symbol:
\[\mathcal P_n\]
Definition:

For an integer $n\ge1$, write $[n]=\{1,\ldots,n\}$ and $Q_n=\{0,1\}^{[n]}$. Take the disjoint union $X_n=[n]\sqcup\{p_v:v\in Q_n\}$, where each $p_v$ is a new coordinate. The private-coordinate cube is $\mathcal P_n=\{h_v:v\in Q_n\}\subseteq\{0,1\}^{X_n}$, with $h_v(i)=v(i)$ for $i\in[n]$ and $h_v(p_w)=1$ if and only if $v=w$. Thus each concept has a private positive coordinate, and projection onto the original $n$ coordinates is the full cube.

Comments:

An explicit private-coordinate realization of the separation mechanism used in [KSZ2019], Propositions 26 and 28(1), which credit Goldman--Kearns for the teaching/VC separation. The exact projected-NCTD and compression identities here are proved directly in the database; no novelty claim is made.
