Antichain Number

Name: Antichain Number

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{AN}\]
Definition:

Fix a finite ambient domain $X$ and a binary class $\mathcal H\subseteq\{0,1\}^{X}$. An antichain teacher mapping assigns every $h\in\mathcal H$ a labeled sample $T(h)\subseteq X\times\{0,1\}$ consistent with $h$, such that for distinct $h,g\in\mathcal H$ neither $T(h)\subseteq T(g)$ nor $T(g)\subseteq T(h)$. The antichain number $\mathrm{AN}(\mathcal H)$ is the minimum, over such mappings, of $\max_{h\in\mathcal H}|T(h)|$, with value zero for an empty class. Inclusion is inclusion of labeled samples, not just of their coordinate domains. The specified ambient domain is part of the input: constant coordinates may be used and must not be discarded automatically. Subfamily monotonicity keeps this domain fixed; projection and conditioning use their resulting domains.

Description:

A batch-teaching parameter based on antichain mappings of labeled samples.

Origin References:

The antichain number is defined and studied in [simon2023map]; its VC upper bound is attributed there to [mansouri2022batch].
