Balbach Teaching Dimension

Name: Balbach Teaching Dimension

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{BTD}\]
Definition:

For $h\in\mathcal H$, set $\mathrm{BTD}^{0}(h,\mathcal H)$ to its ordinary minimum teaching-set size. Inductively, $\mathrm{BTD}^{k+1}(h,\mathcal H)$ is the minimum teaching-set size of $h$ inside the subfamily of hypotheses whose $k$th value is at least that size. Once these values stabilize, the Balbach teaching dimension is their maximum over $h\in\mathcal H$.

Description:

An iteratively defined teaching parameter. It is monotone under subclasses, lies between recursive and ordinary teaching dimension, and is incomparable with subset teaching dimension.

Origin References:

Introduced in [balbach2008measuring]; its basic comparisons and subclass monotonicity are proved in [zilles2011models].
