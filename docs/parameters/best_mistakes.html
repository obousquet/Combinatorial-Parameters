Best Mistakes

Name: Best Mistakes

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{best}}\]
Definition:

The Best Mistakes parameter is the smallest worst-case number of prediction mistakes achievable when the learner may choose a presentation sequence favorable to it, while the feedback remains consistent with one target concept in $\mathcal H$.

Origin References:

The favorably ordered (best) mistake model is studied in [ben1997online].
