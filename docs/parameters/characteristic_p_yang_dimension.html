Characteristic-p Yang Dimension

Name: Characteristic-p Yang Dimension

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{Y}_p\]
Definition:

For each prime $p$, the characteristic-$p$ Yang dimension is $\mathrm{Y}_p(\mathcal H):=\operatorname{pd}_{\mathbb F_p}(I^*_{\mathcal H})$. Equivalently it is the regularity of the Yang complex over $\mathbb F_p$. Extension of scalars shows that it depends only on the characteristic, not on the particular field of characteristic $p$. For every prime $p$, $\mathrm Y_p\geq\mathrm Y_0$. For a fixed finite class, equality holds for all but finitely many primes; possible exceptional primes divide torsion in an integral homology group occurring in Hochster's formula. The Anchored Torsion Class has strictly larger Yang dimension in characteristic two than in characteristic zero or any odd characteristic. Thus characteristic dependence does occur. The Odd-Torsion Dual Class has the reverse strict inequality $\mathrm Y_3>\mathrm Y_2$. Thus neither characteristic two nor three dominates the other universally. These two finite examples do not establish unbounded-ratio incomparability or settle comparisons for every pair of positive primes.

Description:

Family of Yang dimensions over a field of fixed positive characteristic $p$

Comments:

This database row is a uniformly quantified family, one parameter for each fixed prime $p$. Its $p=2$ specialization is the catalogue's default Yang dimension $\mathrm Y$. A direct lattice-homology census found $\mathrm Y_2=\mathrm Y_3$ for every class on at most four coordinates (up to cube symmetry); this is a bounded computation, not a general theorem.

Origin References:

The underlying homological dimension was introduced by Yang in [yang2018homological]; the coefficient-field notation is made explicit in the local Yang-dimension draft.
