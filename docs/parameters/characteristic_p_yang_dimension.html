Characteristic-p Yang Dimension

Name: Characteristic-p Yang Dimension

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{Y}_p\]
Definition:

For each prime $p$, the characteristic-$p$ Yang dimension is $\mathrm{Y}_p(\mathcal H):=\operatorname{pd}_{\mathbb F_p}(I^*_{\mathcal H})$. Equivalently it is the regularity of the Yang complex over $\mathbb F_p$. Extension of scalars shows that it depends only on the characteristic, not on the particular field of characteristic $p$. For every prime $p$, $\mathrm Y_p\geq\mathrm Y_0$. For a fixed finite class, equality holds for all but finitely many primes; possible exceptional primes divide torsion in an integral homology group occurring in Hochster's formula. Thus the homological formula permits characteristic dependence. No explicit Boolean class with $\mathrm Y_p\ne\mathrm Y_q$ is currently recorded, so neither a strict separation nor an ordering between distinct positive characteristics is asserted.

Description:

Family of Yang dimensions over a field of fixed positive characteristic $p$

Comments:

This database row is a uniformly quantified family, one parameter for each fixed prime $p$. Its $p=2$ specialization is the catalogue's default Yang dimension $\mathrm Y$. A direct lattice-homology census found $\mathrm Y_2=\mathrm Y_3$ for every class on at most four coordinates (up to cube symmetry); this is a bounded computation, not a general theorem.

Origin References:

The underlying homological dimension was introduced by Yang in [yang2018homological]; the coefficient-field notation is made explicit in the local Yang-dimension draft.
