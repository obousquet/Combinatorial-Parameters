Characteristic-zero Yang Dimension

Name: Characteristic-zero Yang Dimension

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{Y}_0\]
Definition:

The characteristic-zero Yang dimension is $\mathrm{Y}_0(\mathcal H):=\operatorname{pd}_{\mathbb Q}(I^*_{\mathcal H})$, equivalently the regularity of the Yang complex over $\mathbb Q$. It has the same value over every characteristic-zero field. It is at most $\mathrm Y_p$ for every prime $p$. For a fixed finite class, $\mathrm Y_p=\mathrm Y_0$ outside the finite set of primes detected by torsion in the integral Hochster/interval complexes.

Description:

Yang dimension over a characteristic-zero field

Origin References:

The underlying homological dimension was introduced by Yang in [yang2018homological]; this characteristic-zero specialization is standard coefficient-field notation.
