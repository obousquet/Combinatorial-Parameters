co-VC dimension

Name: co-VC dimension

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{coVC}\]
Definition:

The coVC number $\mathrm{coVC}(\mathcal H)$ is the largest $|S|$ for which some $S\subseteq\mathcal X$ and $T\subseteq S$ satisfy $$T\notin\mathcal H_{|S}\quad\text{and}\quad T\mathbin{\triangle}\{x\}\in\mathcal H_{|S}\quad\text{for every }x\in S.$$ Such a configuration is a hollow star.

Description:

The maximum dimension of a hollow star in a coordinate projection. It is also called the hollow star number, dual Helly number, or consistency dimension (up to the conventional shift used for Helly numbers).

Comments:

Also called the consistency dimension: it is the least $k$ such that every nonmember admits a labeled sample of size at most $k$ inconsistent with the class. This is equivalent to the hollow-star/co-VC formulation. For intersection-closed or extremal classes (hence maximum classes), $\mathrm{coVC}\leq\mathrm{VC}+1$; affine halfspaces in $\mathbb R^d$ with both constants attain $d+2=\mathrm{VC}+1$.

Origin References:

Introduced as co-VC dimension in [kane2019communication]; the equivalent dual Helly number and hollow-star formulation are developed in [bousquet2020proper]. The consistency-dimension terminology appears in [hegedHus1995generalized].
