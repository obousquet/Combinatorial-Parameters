Dual Antipodal VC Dimension

Name: Dual Antipodal VC Dimension

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{VC}^{*a}\]
Definition:

A set of hypotheses $A\subseteq\mathcal H$ is dually antipodally shattered if, for every labeling $s:A\to\{0,1\}$, some point $x$ induces either $s$ or its bitwise complement on $A$. The dual antipodal VC dimension $\mathrm{VC}^{*a}(\mathcal H)$ is the largest size of such a set; equivalently it is the antipodal VC dimension of $\mathcal H^*$.

Description:

Antipodal relaxation of dual VC dimension; distinct from the older parameter named dual sign rank, which is antipodal VC dimension rather than its dual.

Origin References:

The antipodal-shattering formulation and its dual are used explicitly in [chornomaz2025spherical]; antipodal VC was introduced under the name dual sign rank in [alon2016sign].
