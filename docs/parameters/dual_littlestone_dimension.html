Dual Littlestone Dimension

Name: Dual Littlestone Dimension

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{L}^{*}\]
Definition:

For a finite binary class $\mathcal H$, let $\mathcal H^*=\{x^*:x\in\mathcal X\}$ be its dual class, where $x^*(h)=h(x)$ for $h\in\mathcal H$. The dual Littlestone dimension is $$\mathrm L^*(\mathcal H):=\mathrm L(\mathcal H^*).$$ Equivalently, it is the largest depth of a complete binary tree whose internal nodes are labeled by hypotheses of $\mathcal H$ and whose root-to-leaf label sequences are induced by coordinates of $\mathcal H$.

Description:

The sequential/shattered-tree strengthening of dual VC dimension. For finite nonempty classes on nonempty domains, $\mathrm L^*\le\lfloor\log_2(\mathrm E+2)\rfloor$. The general duality bound $\mathrm L^*\le2^{2^{\mathrm L+2}}-2$ is recorded in Corollary 3.8 of the first arXiv version of [bhaskar2017thicket], and quoted on page 3 of [ghazi2021proper]. Half-intervals have $\mathrm{VC}^*=1$ and unbounded $\mathrm L^*$, so dual VC alone cannot bound dual Littlestone dimension.

Origin References:

Used under this name in [ghazi2021proper]; called dual thicket dimension in [bhaskar2017thicket].
