Dual VC Dimension

Name: Dual VC Dimension

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{VC}^{*}\]
Definition:

For $\mathcal H\subseteq2^{\mathcal X}$, let $\mathcal H^*=\{x^*:x\in\mathcal X\}$ be the dual class on domain $\mathcal H$, where $x^*=\{h\in\mathcal H:x\in h\}$. The dual VC dimension is $\mathrm{VC}^{*}(\mathcal H)=\mathrm{VC}(\mathcal H^*)$.

Description:

The shattering dimension of the transposed incidence matrix.

Origin References:

The dual shatter function and its dimension are classical; the standard VC/dual-VC comparison is due to Assouad, [assouad1983densite].

Category: Shattering
