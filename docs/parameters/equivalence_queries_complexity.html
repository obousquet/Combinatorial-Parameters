Equivalence Queries Complexity

Name: Equivalence Queries Complexity

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{Q}_{\mathrm{eq}}\]
Definition:

The Equivalence Queries Complexity of $\mathcal{H}$ is the minimum worst-case number of equivalence queries needed to identify an unknown target in $\mathcal{H}$, when the learner may propose arbitrary Boolean functions as equivalence hypotheses.

Origin References:

Equivalence queries in the exact query-learning model are introduced in [angluin1988queries].
