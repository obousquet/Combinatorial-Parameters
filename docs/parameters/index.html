Effective Range

Name: Effective Range

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{E}\]
Definition:

For a family $\mathcal{H}\subseteq2^{\mathcal{X}}$, the effective range is $$E(\mathcal{H}):=\bigl|\{x\in\mathcal{X}:\exists h_1,h_2\in\mathcal{H}\text{ with }x\in h_1\mathbin{\triangle}h_2\}\bigr|.$$ Thus it counts the coordinates on which two concepts of $\mathcal H$ disagree.

Threshold Dimension

Name: Threshold Dimension

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{T}\]
Definition:

The threshold dimension $\mathrm T(\mathcal H)$ is the largest $d$ for which some $S=\{x_1,\ldots,x_d\}\subseteq\mathcal X$ has an ordering satisfying $$\{x_1,\ldots,x_k\}\in\mathcal H_{|S}\quad\text{for every }0\leq k\leq d.$$ Equivalently, $\mathcal H_{|S}$ contains a chain of $d+1$ traces.

Comments:

Note that this means $\mathcal{H}\_{|S}$ contains a $d+1$-chain, i.e. $d+1$ sets $S_1,\ldots,S_{d+1}$ such that $S_1\subset \ldots\subset S_{d+1}$.

Origin References:

The threshold-pattern formulation is recorded in [alon2020closure], which attributes the classical Littlestone/threshold connection to Shelah and Hodges.

Densest Subgraph (twice)

Name: Densest Subgraph (twice)

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{dens}^*\]
Definition:

The densest subgraph dimension of a concept class is equal to twice the density of the densest subgraph (a.k.a. subgraph density) of its $1$-inclusion graph.

Comments:

The densest subgraph is dual to the lowest outdegree orientation. Conditioning gives an induced subgraph of the $1$-inclusion graph, so this parameter is c-monotonic.

Origin References:

The density and densest-subgraph treatment of the $1$-inclusion graph is part of the random-point prediction framework developed in [haussler1988predicting] [haussler1994predicting].

Double Density or Average Degree

Name: Double Density or Average Degree

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{dens}\]
Definition:

The double-density of a concept class is defined as twice the density of its $1$-inclusion graph, i.e twice the ratio of edges to vertices, or equivalently the average degree.

Comments:

Not c-monotonic: for $\mathcal H=\{000,001,110\}$, the average degree is $2/3$, while conditioning the first coordinate to $0$ leaves an edge of average degree $1$.

The one-inclusion-graph density framework is used in [haussler1988predicting] [haussler1994predicting].

Name: co-VC dimension

Definition:

Information Complexity

Definition:

The Information Complexity of a hypothesis class $\mathcal{H}$ is the supremum, over sample sizes and data distributions, of the least mutual information $I(S;\mathcal{A}(S))$ between a sample $S$ and the output of a PAC-learning algorithm $\mathcal{A}$ for $\mathcal{H}$.

This is an exponential bound, so it is recorded here rather than as one of the graph's affine or logarithmic relationship edges.
