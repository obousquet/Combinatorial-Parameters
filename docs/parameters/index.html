Threshold Dimension

Name: Threshold Dimension

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{T}\]
Definition:

The Threshold dimension of a family $\mathcal{H}$, denoted by $T(\mathcal{H})$, is the largest integer $d$ such that there exists a set $S\subset \mathcal{X}$, of size $d$ such that $\mathcal{H}\_{|S}$ contains thresholds, i.e. there is an ordering $x_1,\ldots,x_d$ of the elements of $S$ such that $$ \forall 0\le k\le d,\, \{x_1,\ldots,x_k\} \in \mathcal{H}\_{|S}\,. $$

Comments:

Note that this means $\mathcal{H}\_{|S}$ contains a $d+1$-chain, i.e. $d+1$ sets $S_1,\ldots,S_{d+1}$ such that $S_1\subset \ldots\subset S_{d+1}$.

The densest subgraph dimension of a concept class is equal to twice the density of the densest subgraph (a.k.a. subgraph density) of its $1$-inclusion graph.

Yang dimension

Name: Yang dimension

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{Y}\]
Definition:

The Yang Dimension in this catalogue is the characteristic-two specialization $\mathrm{Y}(\mathcal H)=\mathrm{Y}_2(\mathcal H)$: the projective dimension, over $\mathbb F_2$, of its associated ideal $I^*_{\mathcal H}$; equivalently, the length of a minimal free resolution over $\mathbb F_2$. Characteristic zero gives a lower bound $\mathrm Y_0\leq\mathrm Y_2$. The homological formula permits exceptions at torsion primes, but no explicit Boolean class separating $\mathrm Y_2$ from another characteristic is currently recorded.

Description:

Yang dimension over the default coefficient field $\mathbb F_2$ (that is, $\mathrm Y_2$)

Comments:

The characteristic-$p$ entry denotes a family of fixed-prime parameters. The default node is its $p=2$ specialization, not a field-independent invariant.

Origin References:

Introduced as homological dimension in [yang2018homological].

Category: Algebraic

Symmetric: Yes

Name: co-VC dimension

\[\mathrm{coVC}\]
Definition:

The coVC number of a family $\mathcal{H}$, denoted by $\covcmod(\mathcal{H})$, is the largest integer $d$ such that there exists a set $S\subset \mathcal{X}$, of size $d$ such that $\mathcal{H}\_{|S}$ contains a hollow $d$-star, i.e. there is a subset $T\subset S$ such that $$ T\notin \mathcal{H}\_{|S} \,\mbox{ and }\, \forall x\in S,\, T\Delta \{x\} \in \mathcal{H}\_{|S}\,. $$

Description:

The maximum dimension of a hollow star in a coordinate projection. It is also called the hollow star number, dual Helly number, or consistency dimension (up to the conventional shift used for Helly numbers).

Comments:

Also called the consistency dimension: it is the least $k$ such that every nonmember admits a labeled sample of size at most $k$ inconsistent with the class. Information Complexity

Name: Information Complexity

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{IC}\]
Definition:

The Information Complexity of a hypothesis class $\mathcal{H}$ is the supremum, over sample sizes and data distributions, of the least mutual information $I(S;\mathcal{A}(S))$ between a sample $S$ and the output of a PAC-learning algorithm $\mathcal{A}$ for $\mathcal{H}$.

Description:

For arbitrary binary classes, finite information complexity is equivalent to finite Littlestone dimension. More quantitatively, finite Littlestone dimension $d$ implies $\mathrm{IC}(\mathcal H)\leq 2^d+\log_2(d+1)+3+3/(e\ln 2)$, by Pradeep, Nachum, and Gastpar. This is an exponential bound, so it is recorded here rather than as one of the graph's affine or logarithmic relationship edges.

Origin References:

Defined in this form by Pradeep, Nachum, and Gastpar [pradeep2022information].
