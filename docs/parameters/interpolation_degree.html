Interpolation degree

Name: Interpolation degree

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{intdeg}\]
Definition:

The Interpolation Degree of $\mathcal{H}$ is the smallest degree needed to interpolate every real-valued function on $\mathcal{H}$. Equivalently, it is the minimum, over bases of $\mathbb{R}[x]/V(\mathcal{H})$, of the largest degree of a basis element.

Description:

Minimum degree of an interpolating polynomial

Origin References:

Introduced as interpolation degree for Boolean-cube subsets by Moran and Rashtchian in [moran2015shattered].
