Labeled Sample Compression

Name: Labeled Sample Compression

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{LSC}\]
Definition:

The Labeled Sample Compression of a nonempty finite binary class $\mathcal H\subseteq\{0,1\}^{\mathcal X}$ is the smallest integer $k\ge0$ for which there are fixed maps $\kappa$ and $\rho$ with the following property. For every realizable labeled sample $(S,y)$, meaning $S\subseteq\mathcal X$ and $y=h|_S$ for some $h\in\mathcal H$, the encoder retains $\kappa(S,y)=(U,y|_U)$ with $U\subseteq S$ and $|U|\le k$, and the decoder returns $\rho(U,y|_U)\in\{0,1\}^{\mathcal X}$ agreeing with $y$ on all of $S$. The decoder receives only the retained labeled subset: no auxiliary message, ordering, or repeated examples are allowed. Reconstruction may be outside $\mathcal H$.

Comments:

Some literature counts retained examples plus auxiliary bits as compression size. The exponential theorem in [moran2016compression] uses such messages and cannot be imported with its rate unchanged. Two survey propositions (prop:compression-message-removal and prop:compression-menu-halving) convert kernel size $k$ and $m$ possible messages to message-free labeled size $O(m(k+1)^3)$, or, for $k\ge1$, at most $2(k+\lfloor\log_2m\rfloor+1)^{k+3}$. For $k=0$ the latter construction costs at most $\lfloor\log_2m\rfloor$. This is polynomial in the bit budget for each fixed $k$, not with a degree independent of $k$. The survey corollary (cor:message-free-vc-finite), combined with the source theorem, proves $\mathrm{LSC}\le 2^{2^{O(\mathrm{VC})}}$ for positive VC dimension, and value zero at VC dimension zero. The linear-VC question remains open. Full proofs are survey-owned. No stable or proper VC-bound consequence is asserted.

Origin References:

The standard sample-compression framework is developed in [FW1995].
