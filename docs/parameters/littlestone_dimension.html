Littlestone Dimension

Name: Littlestone Dimension

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{L}\]
Definition:

The Littlestone Dimension of $\mathcal{H}$ is the largest depth of a complete binary tree whose internal nodes are labeled by domain points and for which every root-to-leaf sequence of edge labels is realized by a member of $\mathcal{H}$.
