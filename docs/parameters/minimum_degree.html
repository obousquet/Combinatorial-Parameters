Minimum Degree

Name: Minimum Degree

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{d}_{\min}\]
Definition:

The minimum degree of a concept class is defined as the minimum degree of its $1$-inclusion graph.

Comments:

Not c-monotonic: for $\mathcal H=\{000,001,110\}$, the isolated function $110$ makes the minimum degree $0$, while conditioning the first coordinate to $0$ leaves an edge of minimum degree $1$.
