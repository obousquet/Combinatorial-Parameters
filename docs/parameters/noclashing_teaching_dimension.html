No-Clashing Teaching Dimension

Name: No-Clashing Teaching Dimension

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{NCTD}\]
Definition:

For a teacher map $T$ assigning every $h\in\mathcal H$ a labeled sample $T(h)$ consistent with $h$, a pair $h\ne h'$ clashes when $T(h)$ is consistent with $h'$ and $T(h')$ is consistent with $h$. The No-Clashing Teaching Dimension is $\min_T\max_{h\in\mathcal H}|T(h)|$ over teacher maps with no clash.

Origin References:

Introduced as the no-clashing teaching dimension in [KSZ2019]. This record uses the standard mutual-consistency definition; it makes no claimed VC-dimension upper bound.
