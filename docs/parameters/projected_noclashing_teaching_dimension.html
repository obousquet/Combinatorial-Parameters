Name: Projected No-Clashing Teaching Dimension

Definition:

The Projected No-Clashing Teaching Dimension is $$\mathrm{NCTD}^{p}(\mathcal H)\bydef\max_{S\subseteq\mathcal X}\mathrm{NCTD}(\mathcal H_{|S}),$$ where $\mathrm{NCTD}$ is the no-clashing teaching dimension of the projected trace class.

Description:

The projection-monotonic closure of no-clashing teaching dimension. It is the quantity appearing in the stable-compression lower-bound theorem of the survey.
