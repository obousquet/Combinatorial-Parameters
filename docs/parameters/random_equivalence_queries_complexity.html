Random Equivalence Queries Complexity

Name: Random Equivalence Queries Complexity

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{Q}_{\mathrm{req}}\]
Definition:

For a fixed known probability distribution $\mu$ on the domain, assumed to give positive mass to every relevant disagreement set, the Random Equivalence Queries Complexity is the least worst-case expected number of arbitrary equivalence queries needed to identify a target in $\mathcal H$, when a failed hypothesis receives a counterexample sampled from $\mu$ conditioned on its disagreement set with the target. Thus the quantity is properly written $\mathrm Q_{\mathrm{req}}(\mathcal H;\mu)$.

Comments:

This is distribution-dependent rather than a pure invariant of $\mathcal H$. No projection or conditioning comparison is asserted until a convention for transporting $\mu$ is fixed.

Origin References:

Introduced by Angluin and Dohrn in [angluin2017random].
