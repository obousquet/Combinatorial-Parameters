Recursive Teaching Dimension

Name: Recursive Teaching Dimension

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{RTD}\]
Definition:

The Recursive Teaching Dimension of $\mathcal{H}$ is the least $d$ for which its concepts can be removed in successive nonempty batches, each concept in a batch having a teaching set of size at most $d$ with respect to the concepts not removed earlier.

Origin References:

Introduced as a teaching model in [zilles2011models]; its relation to VC dimension and sample compression is developed in [doliwa2014recursive].

Category: Teaching and Hitting
