SCO Dimension

Name: SCO Dimension

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{SCO}\]
Definition:

The SCO dimension $\mathrm{SCO}(\mathcal H)$ is the least $d$ for which, for some $\alpha>0$ and $0<\beta<1/2$, realizable learning of $\mathcal H$ admits an $(\alpha,\beta)$-reduction to a $d$-dimensional stochastic convex optimization problem: approximately optimizing its convex loss decodes to a hypothesis of error at most $\beta$.

Description:

A reduction-based learning dimension; the reduction permits fixed positive optimization slack and error below one half.

Origin References:

Defined in the SCO-reduction framework summarized in [chornomaz2025spherical].
