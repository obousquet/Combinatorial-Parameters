Spherical Dimension

Name: Spherical Dimension

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{sd}\]
Definition:

For a finite binary class $\mathcal H$, let $\Delta_{\mathcal H}$ be its space of realizable distributions on labeled examples and let $\Delta_{\mathcal H}^{\mathrm{ant}}=\Delta_{\mathcal H}\cap(-\Delta_{\mathcal H})$, where $-$ flips labels. The spherical dimension $\mathrm{sd}(\mathcal H)$ is the largest $d$ for which there is a continuous antipodality-preserving map $S^d\to\Delta_{\mathcal H}^{\mathrm{ant}}$.

Description:

Finite-class version only; the distinct finitely supported, simplicial, and infinitely supported variants are intentionally not catalogued here.

Origin References:

Introduced by Chornomaz, Moran, and Waknine in [chornomaz2025spherical].
