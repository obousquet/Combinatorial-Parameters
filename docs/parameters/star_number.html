Star number

Name: Star number

Symbol:
\[\mathrm{S}\]
Definition:

The star number $\mathrm S(\mathcal H)$ is the largest $|S|$ for which some $S\subseteq\mathcal X$ and $T\subseteq S$ satisfy $$T\in\mathcal H_{|S}\quad\text{and}\quad T\mathbin{\triangle}\{x\}\in\mathcal H_{|S}\quad\text{for every }x\in S.$$

Origin References:

Introduced as the combinatorial measure called the star number in [hanneke2015minimax].
