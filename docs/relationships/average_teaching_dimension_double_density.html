Average Teaching Dimension / Double Density

Name: Average Teaching Dimension / Double Density

Parameter 1: Average Teaching Dimension

Parameter 2: Double Density or Average Degree

Relationship Type: $P_1\ge P_2$

Variant:

Details:

For every finite binary class, average teaching dimension is at least the average degree of its $1$-inclusion graph.

Witness: Warmuth's $C_5$ class

Witness Strength: Strict separation

Witness Verification:

For Warmuth's $C_5$ class, the established average teaching dimension is $3$ and its average degree is $1$.

Status: Established

Proof or Proof Sketch:

For each concept $h$, every teaching set has size at least the degree of $h$ in the $1$-inclusion graph. Averaging this pointwise inequality over $h$ gives $\mathrm{TS}_{\mathrm{avg}}(\mathcal H)\ge |\mathcal H|^{-1}\sum_h\deg(h)$, and the right-hand side is the double density (average degree).

Full Proof:

Corollary \ref{cor:average-teaching-density} in the survey, averaging Lemma \ref{lem:teaching-set-degree-lower-bound}.
