Diameter / Sign Rank (affine)

Name: Diameter / Sign Rank (affine)

Parameter 1: Diameter

Parameter 2: Sign Rank

Relationship Type: $P_1\ge cP_2-d$

Additive Constant: 1/2

Multiplicative Constant: 1/2

Variant:

Details:

For every finite binary concept class, $\mathrm{SR}(\mathcal H)\leq2\mathrm D(\mathcal H)+1$.

Witness: Trivial concepts

Witness Strength: Unbounded gap

Witness Verification:

For $\mathcal T_n=\{\emptyset,[n]\}$, diameter is $n$ while sign rank is $1$, so no reverse affine-linear bound holds.

Status: Established

Proof or Proof Sketch:

Fix $h_0\in\mathcal H$ and flip every sign-matrix column on which $h_0$ is positive. The row for $h$ is then positive exactly on $S_h=h\triangle h_0$, whose size is at most $D=\mathrm D(\mathcal H)$. Assign distinct real numbers $t_x$ to the coordinates. If $S_h$ is neither empty nor the whole domain, choose $0<\varepsilon_h<\min_{x\notin S_h}\prod_{s\in S_h}(t_x-t_s)^2$ and use the polynomial $p_h(t)=\varepsilon_h-\prod_{s\in S_h}(t-t_s)^2$. It is positive exactly at the $t_s$ for $s\in S_h$ and has degree at most $2D$. Constant negative and positive polynomials handle $S_h=\emptyset$ and the full domain. The evaluation matrix $(p_h(t_x))$ factors through the $2D+1$ monomials $1,t,\ldots,t^{2D}$, so it has rank at most $2D+1$ and realizes the flipped sign pattern. Column flips preserve sign rank.
