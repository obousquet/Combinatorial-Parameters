Effective Range / Yang dimension

Name: Effective Range / Yang dimension

Parameter 1: Effective Range

Parameter 2: Yang dimension

Relationship Type: $P_1\ge P_2$

Variant:

Details:

Dimension bound from Yang's canonical suboplex.

Witness: Trivial concepts

Status: Established

Proof or Proof Sketch:

Coordinates outside the effective range are constant on the class and may be deleted. On the remaining $\mathrm{E}(\mathcal H)$ coordinates, Yang's canonical suboplex is a subcomplex of the boundary of the corresponding cross-polytope; every relevant section has dimension at most $\mathrm{E}(\mathcal H)-1$. Since Yang dimension is one plus the largest possible homology dimension, $\mathrm{Y}(\mathcal H)\le \mathrm{E}(\mathcal H)$.

Minimum Teaching Set Size / Effective VC Radius

Name: Minimum Teaching Set Size / Effective VC Radius

Parameter 1: Minimum Teaching Set Size

Parameter 2: Effective VC Radius

Relationship Type: $P_1\ge P_2$

Variant:

Details:

For finite classes, by the definition of effective VC radius.

Witness: Warmuth's $C_5$ class

Status: Established

Proof or Proof Sketch:

Let $d=\mathrm{VCR}(\mathcal H)$ and let $T$ be a teaching set of size less than $d$; coordinates outside the effective range may be discarded. Extend $T$ to a $d$-subset $S$ of the effective range. Since $S$ is shattered, there is a concept other than the target that agrees with it on $T$ and differs on a point of $S\setminus T$, contradicting that $T$ teaches the target. Status:

Littlestone Dimension / Stable Unlabeled Complexity

Details:

Status:

Proof or Proof Sketch:

Use a conservative online learner with mistake bound $\mathrm L(\mathcal H)$, such as the standard optimal algorithm, and cycle it through a realizable sample until a full pass is mistake-free. Retain only the examples on which it made a mistake. There are at most $\mathrm L(\mathcal H)$; their labels are recoverable from the learner's successive mistaken predictions, and removing any unretained example leaves the final predictor unchanged.
