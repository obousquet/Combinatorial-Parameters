Name: Projected Recursive Teaching Dimension / Projected Minimum Degree

Parameter 1: Projected Recursive Teaching Dimension

Parameter 2: Projected Minimum Degree

Relationship Type: $P_1\ge P_2$

Variant:

Details:

For every finite binary class, projected recursive teaching dimension dominates projected minimum degree.

Witness: Warmuth's $C_5$ class

Witness Strength: Strict separation

Witness Verification:

For Warmuth's $C_5$ class, the established values are $\mathrm{RTD}^p=3$ and $\mathrm d^p_{\min}=2$.

Status: Established

Proof or Proof Sketch:

Apply the recursive-teaching/degeneracy inequality to each coordinate projection $\mathcal H_{|T}$. Its degeneracy is at least its minimum degree, so $\mathrm{RTD}(\mathcal H_{|T})\ge\mathrm d_{\min}(\mathcal H_{|T})$. Maximizing over $T$ gives $\mathrm{RTD}^{p}(\mathcal H)\ge\mathrm d_{\min}^{p}(\mathcal H)$.

Full Proof:

Corollary \ref{cor:projected-rtd-projected-min-degree} in the survey, applying Corollary \ref{cor:rtd-degeneracy} to every coordinate projection.
