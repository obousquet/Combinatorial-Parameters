Subset Teaching Dimension / Littlestone Dimension (Incomparable)

Name: Subset Teaching Dimension / Littlestone Dimension (Incomparable)

Parameter 1: Subset Teaching Dimension

Parameter 2: Littlestone Dimension

Relationship Type: $P_1 \parallel P_2$ (incomparable)

Variant:

Details:

Neither parameter admits a universal affine-linear upper bound in terms of the other on finite binary concept classes.

Witness Strength:

Witness: Parameter 1 Larger:

For $n\ge4$, let $\mathcal F_n$ consist of all singleton and doubleton subsets of $[n]$. Its STD is $n-1$, while leaf counting gives Littlestone dimension at most $\lfloor\log_2(n(n+1)/2)\rfloor$. The survey proves the complete minimum-teacher fixed point. Thus STD grows linearly while Littlestone grows at most logarithmically.

Witness: Parameter 2 Larger:

For the half-interval classes Half-intervals with $n\ge2$, STD is one and Littlestone dimension is $\lfloor\log_2n\rfloor$. The latter diverges with the former fixed.

Status: Established

Full Proof:

Proposition \ref{prop:subset-teaching-littlestone-incomparable} in the survey; half-interval endpoint values are database-owned.
