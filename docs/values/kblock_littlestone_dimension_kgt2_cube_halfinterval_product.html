Name: $k$-Block Littlestone Dimension of the Cube–Half-interval Product

Class: Cube–Half-interval Product

Parameter: $k$-Block Littlestone Dimension ($k>2$)

Value: $m+\min\{\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor,\lfloor m/(k-1)\rfloor+1\}$

Order: $\Omega(1)$

Details: For $m\ge1,n\ge2$. Block and prefix formulas hold for fixed integer $k\ge2$; the general block catalogue entry uses $k>2$. Asymptotic orders refer to $n\to\infty$ with $m,k$ fixed.

Status: Established

Proof or Derivation:
