Prefix-$k$ Littlestone Dimension of Warmuth's $C_5$ Class

Name: Prefix-$k$ Littlestone Dimension of Warmuth's $C_5$ Class

Class: Warmuth's $C_5$ class

Parameter: Prefix-$k$ Littlestone Dimension

Value: $\begin{cases}3&k\le2,\\2&k\ge3.\end{cases}$

Order: $\Omega(1)$

Status: Established

Proof or Derivation:

For k=1 this is ordinary Littlestone dimension. For k=2, the recorded depth-three Littlestone witness has a common coordinate at its second level and is a prefix-2 tree. For k at least three, depth three would be level-constant on its first three levels and would shatter three coordinates, contradicting VC dimension two. The VC witness gives depth two.

Directly from the prefix-tree definition and the $C_5$ calculations in [littlestone1988learning] [palvolgyi2021unlabeled].
