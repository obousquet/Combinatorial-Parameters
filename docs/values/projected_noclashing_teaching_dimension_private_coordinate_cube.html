Projected No-Clashing Teaching Dimension of Private-Coordinate Cubes

Name: Projected No-Clashing Teaching Dimension of Private-Coordinate Cubes

Class: Private-Coordinate Cubes

Parameter: Projected No-Clashing Teaching Dimension

Value: $\lceil n/2\rceil$

Order: $\Omega(n)$

Status: Established

Proof or Derivation:

Projection onto the original coordinates gives $Q_n$, with NCTD $\lceil n/2\rceil$ by [KSZ2019], Theorem 19. For the reverse bound consider an arbitrary projection retaining original coordinates $S\subseteq[n]$ and some private coordinates. Each concept with a retained private positive has a unique projected trace; assign it that singleton positive teacher. All other distinct traces have zero on every retained private coordinate and are distinguished by their restrictions to $S$. Use a no-clashing assignment of size at most $\lceil |S|/2\rceil$ for this subfamily of the full cube on $S$ (subclass monotonicity and Theorem 19). Two private-positive traces are separated by their private teachers; a private-positive trace and a zero-private trace are separated by the former's positive teacher; pairs of zero-private traces are separated by the chosen cube assignment. If $S$ is empty there is at most one zero-private trace and its teacher is empty. The width is at most $\max(1,\lceil |S|/2\rceil)\le\lceil n/2\rceil$ for $n\ge1$.

References:

Direct projection calculation above, using the full-cube value in [KSZ2019], Theorem 19. This refinement is not attributed to Proposition 28. Regression: sync/verify_private_coordinate_cube.py.
