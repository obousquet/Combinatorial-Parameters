Proper Labeled Sample Compression of Full Cube

Name: Proper Labeled Sample Compression of Full Cube

Class: Full Cube

Parameter: Proper Labeled Sample Compression

Value: $\Theta(n)$

Order: $\Omega(n)$

Details: Let $b_n=\min\{k\ge0:\sum_{i=0}^k2^i\binom ni\ge2^n\}$. Writing $n=5q+r$ with $0\le r<5$, we have $b_n\le\mathrm{pLSC}(2^{[n]})\le2q+\lceil r/2\rceil$. Exact for $n=1,2,3,4,5$: $1,1,2,2,2$; also exact at $n=7$, with value three. No exact general formula is asserted.

Status: Established

Proof or Derivation:

Use the four-case scheme of [darnstadt2016order], Example 2 in the cached August 2014 preprint. On a full cube of dimension $m\ge4$, fix an order of coordinates. (1) If a sample has no positive examples, retain the empty set and reconstruct all zeros. (2) If it has positive examples but no negatives, retain its first positive and reconstruct all ones. (3) If it has exactly one positive and at least one negative, retain that positive and the first negative; reconstruct one only at the retained positive coordinate. (4) Otherwise retain all examples of the minority label, choosing negatives in a tie; reconstruct their label on retained coordinates and the opposite label elsewhere. In case (4), a positive retained set has at least two elements, so it cannot be confused with case (2). A nonempty negative retained set distinguishes case (4) from case (1); a mixed pair identifies case (3). Thus reconstruction depends only on the labeled key, without any auxiliary message, order or repetition. The first three cases use at most two examples, and the last at most $\lfloor m/2\rfloor$, so the width is at most $\lfloor m/2\rfloor$. Every reconstruction belongs to the full cube. In particular the five-cube has width two. One example cannot suffice there: eleven empty/singleton labeled keys cannot decode 32 distinct full inputs. For $n=5q+r$, apply this scheme separately to $q$ fixed five-coordinate blocks and the database-owned stable pair scheme to the remaining $r<5$ coordinates. The decoder recovers each block's key by restriction to its fixed domain. This gives $2q+\lceil r/2\rceil$ without a decomposition message, as in the source's product Corollary 11 and Lemma 12. No stability is asserted for the four-case scheme. For the lower bound, the $2^n$ full inputs need different retained keys, of which there are $\sum_{i=0}^k2^i\binom ni$. This gives $b_n$ and matches the stated upper bound for $n=1,2,3,4,5,7$. For all $n$, $\sum_{i=0}^k2^i\binom ni\le4^k\sum_{i=0}^n2^{-i}\binom ni=4^k(3/2)^n$, so $k\ge(n/2)\log_2(4/3)>n/5$. Ordinary and proper labeled compression coincide on cubes because every binary total reconstruction is a concept. The solver-free checker sync/verify_full_cube_labeled_compression.py replays the four cases and their fixed products.
