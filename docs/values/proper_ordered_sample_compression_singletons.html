Proper Ordered Sample Compression of Singletons

Name: Proper Ordered Sample Compression of Singletons

Class: Singletons

Parameter: Proper Ordered Sample Compression

Value: $n-1$

Order: $\Omega(n)$

Details: The proper order-compression number of the singleton class is the separating example between proper and improper order compression.

Status: Established

Proof or Derivation:

Fix any order of the singleton hypotheses. For the lower bound, take the last hypothesis and the all-negative sample on the other $n-1$ coordinates. Each earlier hypothesis is excluded only by the negative observation at its own coordinate. Omitting any of these observations makes an earlier hypothesis consistent, so all $n-1$ are necessary to preserve the first consistent output. For the upper bound when $n\ge2$, a sample with a positive observation retains that observation. For an all-negative sample, let $h_r$ be its first consistent singleton in the fixed order; retain the negative coordinates of the preceding $r-1$ hypotheses. Those observations belong to the sample and determine $h_r$ as its first consistent output. The largest key size is $n-1$. For $n=1$, the empty key reconstructs the sole concept.
