Recursive Teaching Dimension of Chen–Cheng–Tang Teaching Products

Name: Recursive Teaching Dimension of Chen–Cheng–Tang Teaching Products

Class: Chen–Cheng–Tang Teaching Products

Parameter: Recursive Teaching Dimension

Value: $5n$

Order: $\Omega(n)$

Details: For every integer $n\ge1$; $n$ is the number of twelve-coordinate blocks. The ratio $\mathrm{RTD}/\mathrm{VC}=5/3$ is constant, despite the growing additive gap.

Status: Established

Proof or Derivation:

For every finite class, minimum teaching size is at most RTD, which is at most teaching dimension. The minimum-teaching and teaching-dimension values for this product family are both $5n$, giving equality. The construction and ordinary RTD/VC product values are Figure 4 and Section 3 of [chen2016recursive].

References:

[chen2016recursive], Section 3 and Figure 4; [doliwa2014recursive], Lemma 16. Definition-driven finite verification: sync/verify_chen_teaching_products.py and sync/chen_teaching_products.json.
